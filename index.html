About the company

A single-purpose property company in Western Sydney

RAAD PROPERTY ACQUISITION NO 65 PTY LTD is an Australian private company based in Parramatta NSW 2150 (ABN 19 611 683 517, ACN 611 683 517). Its ABN has been active since 19 April 2016.

The company owns the development site at 54-68 Hampstead Road and 276-282 Parramatta Road, Auburn NSW 2144, in the Cumberland Council area. Planning documents for the site name the company as the landowner.

The site has a concept plan approval and two stage approvals for seven-storey buildings with retail, hotel, office, childcare and food and drink uses. This website sets out those approvals as they appear in public NSW planning records.
